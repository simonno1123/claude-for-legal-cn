## Security Review Design Principles

### MMCRG-SDG-001 Authorization Before Review

No Security Review action may occur before an applicable Project Owner authorization is active.

### MMCRG-SDG-002 Sole Positive Authority

Only the Project Owner may create positive authority, accept an asset or authorize downstream action.

### MMCRG-SDG-003 Handoff Is Not Review

This Handoff defines a future review boundary; it does not perform Security Review.

### MMCRG-SDG-004 Security Domain Is Not Finding

Listing a Security Domain does not establish a vulnerability, control failure or finding.

### MMCRG-SDG-005 Control Name Is Not Control Existence

Naming an expected security control does not assert that the control exists, is configured or is effective.

### MMCRG-SDG-006 Finding Is Not Exploit

A future finding does not authorize exploitation, probing, test execution or remediation.

### MMCRG-SDG-007 Exact Asset Identity

Every future review must bind asset name, exact SHA, scope and candidate status.

### MMCRG-SDG-008 Evidence Before Conclusion

No security conclusion may be issued without authorized, accessible and attributable evidence.

### MMCRG-SDG-009 Access Limitation Fidelity

Unavailable or partially available sources must be stated as not independently verified.

### MMCRG-SDG-010 Reviewer Identity Binding

Reviewer role, provider, model where applicable and independence class must be explicit.

### MMCRG-SDG-011 Internal / External Separation

Internal review and Cross-Vendor external review remain separate evidence channels.

### MMCRG-SDG-012 No Timestamp Fabrication

Model-generated or predicted timestamps cannot establish authorization or temporal lineage.

### MMCRG-SDG-013 Provenance Preservation

Manual, system-observed and connector-observed provenance must remain distinguishable.

### MMCRG-SDG-014 No Security Certification

Security Review PASS cannot be represented as certification, absence of vulnerability, safety or deployment readiness.

### MMCRG-SDG-015 Authentication and Least Privilege

Identity, authentication, authorization and least privilege must remain distinct future review concerns.

### MMCRG-SDG-016 Credential Absolute Isolation

Credentials, secrets and keys must not appear in prompts, reports, logs, caches, packets or evidence.

### MMCRG-SDG-017 Integrity Is Not Semantic Correctness

Hash or structural integrity does not establish semantic correctness, authority, safety or truth.

### MMCRG-SDG-018 G0–G5 Preservation

Highest-class composition, UNKNOWN / MIXED blocking, G4 external exclusion and G5 absolute exclusion remain mandatory.

### MMCRG-SDG-019 Provider and Network Binding

Provider, model, endpoint, transport and destination identity must be bound before any future external path may be evaluated or used.

### MMCRG-SDG-020 Minimal Security Evidence

Security evidence must disclose only the minimum authorized content and must not expose exploit-ready or secret material.

### MMCRG-SDG-021 Human Review Gate

Material ambiguity, disagreement or control-effectiveness uncertainty requires authorized human resolution.

### MMCRG-SDG-022 Fail-Closed Availability

Unavailable reviewer, missing evidence, unknown identity or unsafe access must block rather than degrade silently.

### MMCRG-SDG-023 Revocation and Kill Dominance

Kill, revocation, suspension, withdrawal and supersession override queue, retry, recovery and deployment behavior.

### MMCRG-SDG-024 Assurance Track Separation

Threat, Privacy and Security reviews may cross-reference dependencies but may not substitute for one another.

### MMCRG-SDG-025 Immutable History and No Automatic Escalation

History must remain append-preserved, and no PASS, recommendation or majority vote may activate downstream authority.

## Required Security Domains

All twenty-one domains are mandatory future coverage requirements when within an authorized Security Review scope.

### MMCRG-SD-001 Identity and Principal Binding

Review must evaluate explicit attribution and substitution resistance for human, service, model, provider and component identities.

### MMCRG-SD-002 Authentication Boundary

### MMCRG-SD-003 Authorization and Least Privilege

### MMCRG-SD-004 Credential, Secret and Key Isolation

Review must evaluate exclusion, exposure and lifecycle boundaries without receiving protected material.

### MMCRG-SD-005 Session, Token and Replay Control

### MMCRG-SD-006 Input and Instruction Boundary

### MMCRG-SD-007 Packet Identity and Integrity

### MMCRG-SD-008 Queue Integrity and Dispatch Safety

### MMCRG-SD-009 Ledger and Audit Integrity

### MMCRG-SD-010 Decision Gate and Owner Authenticity

### MMCRG-SD-011 Connector and Provider Identity

Review must evaluate exact Connector, provider, model and destination binding where separately authorized.

### MMCRG-SD-012 Network and Transport Boundary

Review must evaluate endpoint, route, transport protection and boundary assumptions only on authorized descriptions or evidence.

### MMCRG-SD-013 Storage, Cache and Log Protection

Review must evaluate confidentiality, integrity, access, retention and disclosure boundaries where such mechanisms exist.

### MMCRG-SD-014 Error and Information Disclosure

Review must evaluate whether errors, traces, metrics and diagnostics minimize and protect information.

### MMCRG-SD-015 Monitoring and Detection

Review must evaluate authorized monitoring coverage, signal provenance, blind spots and notification routing.

### MMCRG-SD-016 Availability, Rate and Retry Safety

Review must evaluate quota, rate, retry, amplification, delayed work and failure behavior.

### MMCRG-SD-017 Kill, Revocation and Suspension

Review must evaluate activation authority, scope, propagation, evidence preservation and downstream blocking.

### MMCRG-SD-018 Recovery and Rollback Integrity

Review must evaluate reauthorization, identity, SHA, evidence, rollback source and no-silent-recovery.

### MMCRG-SD-019 Dependency and Supply-Chain Integrity

Review must evaluate identity, source, version, provenance, approval and supersession of dependencies.

### MMCRG-SD-020 Build, Artifact, Environment and Deployment Integrity

Review must evaluate build identity, artifact binding, environment separation, promotion and rollback boundaries.

### MMCRG-SD-021 Incident Handling and Evidence Preservation

Review must evaluate containment boundaries, evidence preservation, notification and authorized recovery.

## Security Domain Coverage Rules

A future Security Review must:

- evaluate every authorized in-scope domain;
- mark missing evidence as BLOCKED or NOT EVALUABLE;
- preserve domain-specific limitations;
- distinguish absence of evidence from absence of vulnerability;
- distinguish governance design exposure from implementation exposure;
- distinguish a named control from an implemented control;
- distinguish implementation from effectiveness;
- distinguish theoretical weakness from demonstrated vulnerability;
- preserve scope exclusions and unresolved issues;
- refuse to combine unrelated evidence into a generalized PASS;
- exclude credentials, secrets and exploit-ready content.

No risk score, maturity score, exploitability score, certification formula or aggregate security score is defined.

## Severity, Likelihood and Uncertainty Governance

Severity, likelihood and uncertainty are independent qualitative axes.

This Spec does not create values, thresholds, scores, formulas or automatic priority.

Future authorized Review must:

- tie any severity statement to a defined impact scope and evidence;
- tie any likelihood statement to authorized evidence and assumptions;
- state uncertainty separately;
- avoid converting model confidence into severity or likelihood;
- avoid combining qualitative axes into a numeric score;
- preserve disagreement and missing evidence.

## Security Review Outcome Semantics

| --- | --- |

| FAIL | at least one unresolved blocking defect exists within the authorized scope |
| BLOCKED | authority, identity, SHA, evidence, classification or safe access prevents review or conclusion |

## Fail-Closed Conditions

Future Security Review must block when:

- Project Owner authority is absent, expired, revoked, suspended or ambiguous;
- reviewed asset or SHA is missing or mismatched;
- accepted baseline or Handoff cannot be established;
- reviewer identity, provider, role or independence is unbound;
- source access is partial but represented as complete;
- recorded SHA is represented as independently verified without source access;
- a model supplies its own governance timestamp;
- data class is UNKNOWN or MIXED;
- an external path includes unauthorized G4;
- any path includes G5;
- credential, secret, key or reusable token appears in evidence;
- provider, model, endpoint, destination or transport is unbound;
- required domain evidence is absent;
- named control lacks implementation or effectiveness evidence for the claimed conclusion;
- evidence is incomplete, stale, replayed or superseded;
- response provenance is missing;
- internal and external reports are merged;
- disagreement lacks required Human Review;
- kill, revocation, suspension, withdrawal or supersession is active;
- review availability, quota or safe access fails;
- a historical report is reused under a new authorization;
- outcome semantics are collapsed;
- PASS is used as certification;
- review attempts to authorize remediation, testing or downstream action.

Protective labels are descriptive only and do not create executable constants.

## Kill, Revocation, Withdrawal and Recovery

Kill activation may be authorized to Project Owner or an exact-scope protective operator expressly delegated by Project Owner.

Re-enable after kill is Project Owner-only and non-delegable.

Kill, revocation or suspension must:

- stop affected future review dispatch and retry;
- prevent affected evidence from current governing use without deleting history;
- preserve activation source, scope and evidence;
- prevent automatic recovery.

Request cancellation terminates the request but does not withdraw or delete the asset.

Asset withdrawal removes the asset from current eligibility but preserves history.

Recovery requires:

- new authority where required;
- current identity and SHA;
- current classification and evidence;
- current reviewer binding;
- explicit Project Owner re-enable after kill;
- no silent carryover from stale or superseded records.
