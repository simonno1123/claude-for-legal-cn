## Fail-Closed Carry-Forward

The Result preserves blocking when:

- Project Owner authority is absent, expired, revoked, suspended or ambiguous;
- asset identity or SHA is missing or mismatched;
- purpose, domain, classification, access or exclusions are incomplete;
- data is UNKNOWN / MIXED, unauthorized G4 or any G5;
- credentials, secrets, keys or reusable tokens appear;
- evidence identity, authority, provenance or sufficiency is incomplete;
- reviewer identity, provider, role or independence is unbound;
- a weaker evidence qualification is represented as stronger;
- a model-generated timestamp is treated as governing;
- internal and external reports are merged;
- historical, stale, superseded, replayed or unbound material is reused;
- disagreement lacks required Human Review;
- outcome semantics are collapsed;
- PASS is represented as secure, certified or deployment-ready;
- kill, revocation, suspension, withdrawal or supersession is active;
- safe access, quota or availability fails;
- review attempts to authorize findings, tests, remediation or downstream work.
